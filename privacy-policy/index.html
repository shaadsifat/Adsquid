The short version

  • We collect only what you choose to send us (for example through a form or WhatsApp) and basic website statistics.
  • We use it to reply to you, prepare your free audit or proposal, and deliver the work you hire us for.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

01Who we are

This website, adsquidbd.com, is run by Adsquid, a digital marketing agency. Our head office is at Section-15, Mirpur, Dhaka-1216, Bangladesh, and we also have an office at 2780 Club Cortile Cir, Kissimmee, FL 34746, USA.

Adsquid is responsible for the personal information described in this policy (in data protection terms, we are the "controller"). For any privacy question, email info@adsquidbd.com or call +880 1888 055 999.

This policy covers our website, our forms, and conversations with us by WhatsApp, phone, email or social media. When we handle data inside a client's own accounts while doing their work (for example their ad accounts or customer lists), we do so on that client's behalf and under their instructions, as explained in section 12.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Free audit forms: your name, business name, WhatsApp number, website or page link, monthly ad budget range, your main goal and the service you are interested in.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are asking about and your message.
  • Messages and calls: what you share with us on WhatsApp, by phone, by email or through our social media pages, including any files you send.
  • Job applications: your CV, portfolio, contact details and anything else you choose to send when applying.
  • Clients: billing and contact details, and the logins or account access you give us to do the work.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: like every website, our hosting server records technical data for each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the time and the page that linked you here. We use this to keep the site secure and working.
  • Website statistics: if we turn on analytics tools (see section 4), they record pages visited, time on page, device and browser type, approximate location (country or city) and how you found us.
  • Spam protection: our forms include hidden checks that look at how quickly a form is sent and whether a hidden field was filled, and we count how many messages come from one IP address in a short time. This only helps us block automated spam.

Information we do not want

Please do not send us sensitive information (such as health details, government ID numbers, passwords or card numbers) through our forms or chats. If we need access to an account to do our work, we will ask for partner or admin access instead of your password wherever the platform allows it.

03How we use your information

We use your information only for the purposes below, and we rely on the legal grounds shown in brackets where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply:

  • To reply to your message or call, and to follow up on it (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or steps you asked us to take before a contract).
  • To prepare the free audit, quote or proposal you asked for (steps before a contract).
  • To deliver, manage and invoice the services you hire us for (performance of a contract).
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records (legal obligation).
  • To understand how visitors use the website and to improve it, and to measure our own advertising (your consent where the law requires it, otherwise our legitimate interest).
  • To protect the website, our team and our clients from spam, fraud and misuse (legitimate interest).
  • To review job applications and contact candidates (steps before a contract, or legitimate interest).
  • To send you updates or offers, but only if you are a client or have agreed to receive them. You can stop these at any time (consent or legitimate interest).

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

04Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Here is what this website uses:

  • No cookies of our own for basic use. The site works without setting any cookies. It only uses your browser's session storage to remember a display setting for animations while the tab is open.
  • Analytics and advertising (only when switched on): we may use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (cookies such as _ga) to count visits, and the Meta Pixel (cookie _fbp) to measure and improve our own Facebook and Instagram ads. These load after the page has finished loading. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the EU and UK), we will ask for it before these cookies are set.
  • Google Maps: the map on our Contact page is provided by Google. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receives your IP address.
  • Google Fonts: our typeface (Montserrat) is delivered by Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download it, so Google receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • WhatsApp, Facebook and LinkedIn links: these only send information to those companies when you click them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use private browsing, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. You can manage Meta ads in your Facebook ad preferences.

05Who we share it with

We share personal information only when we need to, and only with:

  • Our own team in Dhaka and in the USA, who see only what they need for their work.
  • Service providers that help us run the business: our website hosting and email provider (which stores form messages and emails), Google (Analytics, Maps, Fonts and Workspace tools), Meta (WhatsApp and the Meta Pixel), and the project, file storage and accounting tools we use. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Advertising and social media platforms, when you are a client and we run campaigns or manage pages on your behalf, as agreed with you.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, auditors and lawyers, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.
  • A new owner, if Adsquid or part of it is ever merged or sold, in which case this policy will continue to protect your information.

We do not sell your personal information for money. If we switch on advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel, some US state laws may treat this as "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can opt out of that at any time by emailing us (see section 9).

06International transfers

We work from Bangladesh and the United States, and some of our service providers store data in other countries. When your information moves between countries, we take reasonable steps to keep it protected, for example by using well-known providers with strong security and, where the GDPR applies, standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.

07How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and free audit requests that do not lead to a project: up to 24 months after our last contact, so we have the history if you come back.
  • Client records: for as long as we work together, then for as long as tax, accounting and other laws require.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, unless you agree to a longer period for future openings.
  • Server logs: for the short period set by our hosting provider, usually a few weeks to a few months.
  • Analytics data: no longer than 14 months.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

08How we protect it

The whole website runs over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Form messages are emailed to our team, and a private backup copy is stored on our server in a folder that cannot be opened from the web. Access to client data is limited to the team members working on that account, and we use strong passwords and two-step verification on our business accounts.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this does not affect what we did before);
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising (for residents of California and other US states with privacy laws), without being treated differently for doing so.

To use any of these rights, email info@adsquidbd.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we will reply within one month (or within the time your local law sets). You can also ask someone to act for you, with your written permission.

If you are in the EU or UK and are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

10Marketing messages

We only send marketing emails or WhatsApp messages to clients and to people who agreed to receive them. Every message tells you how to stop them: reply "STOP" or use the unsubscribe link, and we will remove you.

11Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

12Data we handle for our clients

When a client hires us to run ads, manage social media, build a website or set up software, we may handle personal information that belongs to the client's own customers (for example audience lists, leads or orders). We use that information only to do the client's work and only as they instruct us. The client's own privacy policy applies to it, and we sign a data processing agreement on request.

13Other websites

Our site links to other websites and apps, such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Google Maps. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, tools or the law change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make an important change, we will say so clearly on this page.
