We are a TikTok ads agency in Bangladesh making ads that feel like TikTok, so people watch instead of swiping. Young audiences spend hours on the For You page, and the right creative can reach them at a low cost.

How we make TikTok ads people watch

TikTok rewards ads that look like content, not commercials. Our creative team scripts hook-first videos, shoots them on phones or in studio and edits them with captions, trends and sound. We test several hooks for every offer, because the first two seconds decide everything.

Behind the creatives, we set up the TikTok Pixel and Events API so the platform optimises for real purchases or leads. Spark ads boost your best organic posts or creator videos while keeping their likes and comments. We report cost per result weekly and keep a steady flow of fresh videos, since TikTok creatives tire quickly.

02 / Who it's for

For visual products, young buyers.

05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does TikTok work for my business?

If your buyers are 18-35 and your product is visual, very likely. We will tell you honestly after a quick review.

Can I reuse my Facebook videos?

Sometimes, with a re-edit. Ads that look native to TikTok almost always perform better.

What are Spark ads?

Ads that boost an organic post from your account or a creator, keeping the likes and comments visible.

Do you make the videos?

Yes. Our content team scripts, shoots and edits TikTok-native ads in-house.
