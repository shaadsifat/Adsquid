As a brand identity design agency in Dhaka, we build the logo, the identity system and the guidelines that keep every post, ad and package on brand.

Brand identity design that makes you recognisable

People decide whether to trust a business in seconds, often before they read a word. A clear logo, consistent colours and a confident type style tell them you are serious. Our brand identity design gives your business that first impression, on every screen and every package.

We start with discovery: your story, your customers, your competitors and where you want to be in three years. That research shapes the concepts we present, so every design choice has a reason behind it. You see two or three distinct directions, pick one and refine it with us.

The final delivery is a complete, practical system: logo files for every use, a colour palette, typography, patterns, stationery and a brand guideline book. Your team, printers and future agencies can then apply the brand the same way everywhere, which is how recognition is built.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a brand identity take?

A logo takes about 1-2 weeks. A full identity with guidelines usually takes 3-5 weeks.

How many logo options do I get?

Two or three distinct concepts, then refinements on the one you choose.

What files do I receive?

Vector and web files (AI, SVG, PDF, PNG) in colour, black and white, plus the brand guideline book.

Can you refresh my existing logo?

Yes. Often a careful refresh keeps the recognition you have built and fixes what is not working.
