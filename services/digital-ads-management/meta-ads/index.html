05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Boost post or Ads Manager?

Ads Manager, almost always. It gives you real targeting, testing and sales optimization. Boost is built for reach, not revenue.

Do you set up the Pixel and Conversions API?

Yes. Correct tracking is the first thing we fix, because Meta learns from it.

Can you run ads for my Facebook shop?

Yes. We connect your catalog and run dynamic product ads.

We help you check the reason and file a review, and we set up campaigns to avoid common policy problems.
