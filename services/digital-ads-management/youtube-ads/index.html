05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I pay when people skip?

For skippable in-stream ads you usually pay only when someone watches 30 seconds (or the full ad if shorter) or clicks.

Do I need a big video budget?

No. A clear script and a simple, well-edited video often beat expensive productions.

Can YouTube ads drive sales?

Yes, with conversion tracking and the right campaign type. They also lift your other channels.

Do you make the video?

Yes. Our content team writes, shoots and edits ads built for YouTube.
