We are a social media marketing agency in Bangladesh that keeps your pages active, on brand and answering fast, on every platform your buyers use.

Social media management that builds buyers, not just followers

Your social pages are often the first place a customer checks before they buy. An empty page, a slow reply or a random post tells them to look elsewhere. Our social media marketing keeps every page active, on brand and quick to respond, so people trust you before they ever message.

We start with a simple strategy: who you want to reach, what they care about and which platforms they use. From that we build content pillars and a monthly calendar that mixes posts, carousels, Reels and Stories. You approve everything before the month starts, then our designers, writers and video team produce it.

Publishing is only half the job. We reply to comments and messages within agreed hours, pass sales leads to your team and track what works. Each month you get a report in plain language: reach, engagement, messages and the posts that moved people, plus what we will change next month.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest. We pick the ones where your buyers actually spend time.

How many posts per month?

Most packages include 12 to 30 posts a month, mixed between images, carousels and reels.

Do you reply to messages?

Yes, if you choose community management. We agree on answers to common questions and hand over sales leads to your team.

Do I approve posts first?

Always. You see the full calendar before the month starts and can change anything.
