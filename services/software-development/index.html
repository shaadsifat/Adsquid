Software that fits you.

Our custom software development in Bangladesh replaces spreadsheets and copy-paste with POS, ERP, CRM, HR and accounting tools shaped around how your team works.

Custom software that fits the way you work

Many growing businesses in Bangladesh still run on spreadsheets, notebooks and copy-paste between apps. It works until it does not: stock goes missing, orders are delayed and nobody trusts the numbers. Our custom software development replaces that chaos with one system built around your real workflow.

We start with discovery. We sit with your team, map how work actually happens and find the steps that waste the most time. Then we design screens and flows you can click through before any code is written, so the software fits your team instead of forcing them to change everything.

Development happens in short sprints with a working demo every week or two. You see progress early, give feedback and can adjust priorities. At launch we migrate your data, train your staff and stay on for support. You own the source code and documentation, with no lock-in.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Custom software or a ready-made tool?

If a ready tool fits 90 percent of your needs, use it. Custom makes sense when your process is your advantage.

How long does a project take?

A focused first version usually takes 6-12 weeks. We ship in small steps so you see progress early.

Who owns the code?

You do. Full source code and documentation are handed over on final payment.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly support plans for fixes, updates and new features.
