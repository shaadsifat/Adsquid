POS software in Bangladesh

Sell faster at the counter.

Point of sale software for shops and restaurants, with inventory, receipts and reports built in.

Get a free auditAsk on WhatsApp

Our POS software in Bangladesh helps shops and restaurants bill faster and always know what is in stock. Every sale updates your inventory and reports automatically, so the numbers at the end of the day are ones you can trust.

POS built for local businesses

Cashiers get a simple, fast screen: scan a barcode or tap a product, apply discounts, take cash, card or bKash and print or send a receipt. For restaurants we add table management, kitchen order tickets and split bills. The system can keep working offline and sync when the internet returns.

Owners see sales, profit, top products and staff performance from any device. Stock updates with every sale and purchase, low-stock alerts warn you before items run out, and multiple branches can share one central dashboard. We can also connect your POS to your online store, so stock stays in sync everywhere.

05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The POS can keep billing offline and sync data when the connection returns.

Can it accept bKash and cards?

Yes. We support cash, cards, bKash and other mobile payment methods.

Will it work with my barcode printer?

Usually yes. We support common barcode scanners, receipt printers and label printers.

Can it connect to my online store?

Yes. We can sync stock and orders with WooCommerce or a custom store.
