As a video production agency in Bangladesh, we make reels, motion graphics, promo videos, animation and 3D that hook in the first second and sell by the last.

Video production built for feeds, ads and screens

Video is now the main way people discover brands on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. But most viewers decide to stay or scroll in the first second. Our video production in Bangladesh is built around that moment: a hook first, the message fast and a clear next step at the end.

Our creative director plans every project from the idea down, writing simple scripts and shot lists before anything is filmed or animated. Depending on the goal, we shoot on location in Dhaka, work in a controlled studio setup or create everything in 2D, motion graphics or 3D with no shoot at all.

Every video is delivered in the formats each platform needs: 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 1:1 and 4:5 for feeds and 16:9 for YouTube and websites. Captions are added for people watching on mute, and we can cut several short ads from one longer video, so your budget goes further.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you shoot on location?

Yes, in Dhaka. For many products we can also shoot in our space or create 3D with no shoot at all.

What is a 3D product video?

A 3D animated video of your product. It looks premium, needs no studio and is easy to update.

How long are your reels?

Usually 15-45 seconds. The first second carries the hook; the rest earns the click.

Can the videos be used as ads?

Yes. We deliver 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 versions so every placement is covered.
