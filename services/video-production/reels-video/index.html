Reels that hook in one second.

Vertical videos for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, scripted to hook fast and edited to keep people watching.

Get a free auditAsk on WhatsApp

Our Reels video production in Bangladesh makes short vertical videos that stop the scroll and keep people watching. Reels reach people who do not follow you yet, which makes them the cheapest way to grow on social media.

How we produce Reels that perform

Each Reel starts with a hook: a bold statement, a surprising visual or a question in the first second. We plan short scripts and shot lists in batches, so one filming day can produce a month of content. Formats include tutorials, product reveals, behind-the-scenes, before and after and trend-based ideas.

Editing is where Reels win or lose. We cut fast, add on-screen captions, sound effects and trending audio, and keep every second earning its place. Each Reel is exported in 9:16 with safe zones respected, so nothing important hides behind the platform buttons.

05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long should a Reel be?

Usually 15 to 45 seconds. Shorter Reels often get watched more than once, which helps reach.

Do I need to be on camera?

Not always. Product, process and text-led Reels work well without a presenter.

How many Reels per month?

Eight to sixteen Reels a month is a strong rhythm for most brands.

Can the same Reel go on TikTok?

Yes, with small changes to text and sound. We export versions for each platform.
