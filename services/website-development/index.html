We are a website development company in Bangladesh building fast, SEO-ready websites, from a simple portfolio to a custom platform with local payments built in.

Website development that turns visitors into customers

Your website works 24 hours a day. It answers questions, shows proof and takes orders while you sleep, but only if it is fast, clear and easy to use. Our website development team in Bangladesh builds sites that load quickly on mobile data, guide visitors to one clear action and are simple for your team to update.

Every project starts with planning, not design. We map your pages, features and content, then design mobile-first layouts you can click through before development begins. That way you know exactly what you are getting, and nothing important is forgotten halfway through the build.

Under the hood, each site is built with clean code, technical SEO, analytics and security from day one. We integrate local payment gateways like bKash and SSLCommerz, connect courier and CRM tools where needed and train your team at handover. After launch we offer maintenance plans for updates, backups and small changes.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on pages and features. A portfolio and an e-commerce store are very different. Ask for a free quote with a clear scope.

How long does it take?

A business or portfolio site takes about 2-4 weeks. E-commerce and custom builds take 4-10 weeks.

Can you add bKash payments?

Yes. We integrate bKash, SSLCommerz and other local gateways.

Will I be able to edit the site?

Yes. We build on platforms you can manage, and we train your team at handover.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes: updates, backups, security checks and small changes on a monthly plan.
