Short answer: a successful e-commerce site in Bangladesh needs the right platform, local payments (bKash, Nagad, cards and cash on delivery), courier integration, clear product pages, a short mobile checkout, fast loading, proper tracking and visible trust signals. Use this checklist before you launch or when you rebuild your store.

As a tech specialist, I have seen many stores that look beautiful but lose orders at checkout, or that run ads without any purchase tracking. Moving from Facebook inbox orders to your own store is a big step. This checklist covers what matters most for Bangladeshi buyers.

1. Choose the right platform

PlatformStrengthsWatch out for
WooCommerce (WordPress)Flexible, no platform fee, many local pluginsNeeds good hosting and updates
ShopifyEasy to run, reliable hosting includedMonthly fees, some local integrations need apps
Custom buildExactly your workflow, best performanceHigher upfront cost, needs a technical partner

2. Offer the payments Bangladeshi buyers use

  • bKash and Nagad: the most common digital payments for local buyers.
  • Cards through a gateway: SSLCommerz or similar for Visa, Mastercard and more.
  • Cash on delivery: still expected by many buyers, especially first-time customers.

Consider a small advance payment for COD orders on high-value items. It reduces fake orders and returns without losing serious buyers.

3. Connect delivery properly

Integrate your courier so orders can be booked and tracked without copying details by hand. Show clear delivery charges inside and outside Dhaka, estimated delivery times and your return policy on product pages and at checkout.

4. Build product pages that answer every question

  1. Several clear photos, including close-ups and scale.
  2. Price, discounts and stock status visible at the top.
  3. Sizes, variants and a size guide where needed.
  4. A short description plus detailed specifications.
  5. Delivery time, charges and return policy.
  6. Reviews and real customer photos.
  7. A sticky "Add to cart" or "Buy now" button on mobile.

5. Make checkout short and mobile-first

Every extra field loses buyers. Ask only for what you need: name, phone, address, area and payment method. Allow guest checkout. Use a phone number as the main identifier, because many buyers do not check email. Test the full checkout on a budget Android phone, not just your laptop.

6. Make it fast

7. Set up tracking before running ads

  • Google Analytics 4 with e-commerce events: view item, add to cart, begin checkout, purchase.
  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API, so Facebook and Instagram ads can optimise for purchases.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking and Merchant Center for Shopping ads.

Without purchase tracking, ad platforms optimise for cheap clicks instead of buyers.

8. Show trust everywhere

  • Real contact details, including a phone number and WhatsApp.
  • A clear return and refund policy.
  • Reviews, customer photos and social proof.
  • Secure checkout (HTTPS) and recognisable payment logos.

9. Plan for operations

Decide how stock is updated, who confirms orders, how returns are processed and how you will handle sales days. If you also sell in a shop, connect your store to a POS so stock stays in sync.

The launch checklist

  • Platform chosen and hosting tested
  • bKash, Nagad, cards and COD working
  • Courier connected, delivery charges clear
  • Product pages complete with photos, prices and policies
  • Checkout tested on a budget Android phone
  • Pages loading in about two to three seconds on mobile
  • GA4, Meta Pixel, Conversions API and Google tags firing
  • Trust signals and contact details visible
