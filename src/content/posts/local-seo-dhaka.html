Short answer: local SEO helps people near you find you on Google Search and Maps. For most Dhaka businesses, four things matter most: a complete Google Business Profile, steady reviews, a website with local pages, and the same name, address and phone everywhere.

Step 1: Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

Your profile is your shop window on Google Maps. Claim it at business.google.com, verify it, and fill in every field.

  • Name: your real business name. No extra keywords. Google can suspend profiles for that.
  • Category: pick the most specific primary category, then add a few secondary ones.
  • Address or service area: exact address with the correct map pin. "Mirpur-6" and "Mirpur 6" should not both appear.
  • Hours, phone, website: keep them accurate, including Eid and holiday hours.
  • Photos: real photos of the shop front, team and products. Add new ones every month.

Step 2: Make your details match everywhere

Google checks that your name, address and phone (NAP) match across the web. Use exactly the same format on your website, Facebook page, LinkedIn and directories.

One email domain, one main phone number, one address format. Mixing a Gmail address with your domain email weakens trust.

Step 3: Get reviews, every week

Reviews are one of the strongest local ranking signals, and they decide who gets the call.

  1. Create a short review link from your profile.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp right after a good experience.
  3. Reply to every review, good or bad, within a day or two.

Never buy reviews. Google removes fake reviews and can restrict your profile.

Step 4: Build local pages on your website

If you serve several areas, create a useful page for each main service, and mention the areas you cover naturally. A clinic might have pages for each treatment, with its Mirpur address, map and parking details.

Add LocalBusiness schema with your address, phone, opening hours and coordinates. It helps Google connect your site to your Maps listing.

Step 5: Speed up your site for mobile

Most local searches happen on phones, often on mobile data. A slow site loses the visitor before they call. Aim for pages that load in under 2.5 seconds.

Step 6: Earn local links and mentions

Get listed in trusted Bangladeshi directories and industry associations. Local news mentions, sponsorships and partner websites all help Google trust that you are a real Dhaka business.

Step 7: Post and track

Post offers and updates on your profile every week. Then track calls, direction requests and website clicks in the profile's performance section. Grow what works.
