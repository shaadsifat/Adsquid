Short answer: on-page SEO means making each important page clear to Google and convincing to visitors. Give every page one main keyword, a strong title, logical headings, content that fully answers the search, internal links, optimised images and structured data. This checklist walks through 15 fixes you can apply today.

Most websites we audit in Bangladesh have good businesses behind them and weak pages in front of them. Service pages with 80 words. Titles that say "Home". Images named "IMG_2034.jpg". The good news: on-page SEO is fully under your control, and it is often where the fastest ranking gains are hiding.

Part 1: Keywords and planning

1. Map one main keyword to each page

List your important pages, then give each one a single main keyword. Your Facebook ads service page targets "facebook ads agency in Bangladesh". Your website page targets "website development company in Bangladesh". Two pages should never target the same phrase, or they compete with each other.

2. Check search intent before you write

Search the keyword on Google and look at the top results. Are they service pages, blog guides or product listings? Match that format. If Google shows guides, a sales page will struggle to rank, and the reverse is also true.

3. Include local and Bangla variations

Add your city or area where it matters, such as "in Dhaka" or "in Mirpur". For some services, people search in Bangla. Use those terms naturally in headings or FAQs instead of forcing them everywhere.

4. Write titles that win the click

Your title is your ad on Google. Put the keyword first, add a reason to click and keep your brand name at the end.

5. Write a unique meta description for every page

Google does not always use it, but when it does, a clear description lifts clicks. Duplicate descriptions across pages waste that chance.

6. Use one H1 and a clean heading structure

One H1 that states the topic, then H2s for each main section. Headings help readers skim and help Google understand the page.

Part 3: Content that deserves to rank

7. Answer the full question

A service page should explain what you do, who it is for, how it works, what it costs or how pricing works, and what to do next. Aim for depth, not word count. Thin pages rarely beat competitors who explain more clearly.

8. Put the keyword in the first paragraph

Mention the main keyword naturally in the opening lines. It confirms the topic to Google and to the visitor at the same time.

9. Add FAQs from real customer questions

Your sales team and inbox are full of questions. Answer the common ones on the page. FAQs capture long-tail searches and remove doubts before people call.

Part 4: Links, images and code

10. Link related pages to each other

Internal links guide visitors and pass authority. Link your blog posts to the matching service pages, and link service pages to each other where they make sense.

11. Optimise every image

  • Descriptive file names: facebook-ads-dashboard.webp, not IMG_2034.jpg.
  • Alt text that describes the image for people who cannot see it.
  • Modern formats like WebP and sizes that match the layout.

12. Add structured data

Schema markup tells Google what a page is: a service, product, article, FAQ or local business. It can earn rich results such as FAQ dropdowns or star ratings.

13. Make the page fast on mobile

Most visitors in Bangladesh browse on phones and mobile data. Compress images, remove unused plugins and scripts, and test with PageSpeed Insights. A slow page loses rankings and customers.

14. Show trust signals

Client logos, real project photos, team names and clear contact details all help people trust the page. They also support the experience and expertise signals Google looks for.

15. Give every page one clear next step

A call button, a WhatsApp link or a short form. SEO brings the visitor; the page has to turn them into a lead.

How to prioritise the checklist

  1. Start with pages that already get some impressions in Google Search Console.
  2. Fix titles, H1s and thin content on your top service pages first.
  3. Then add internal links from blog posts to those pages.
  4. Finish with images, schema and speed across the whole site.

Track every change. Note the date you updated a page and watch its impressions, clicks and average position in Search Console over the next four to eight weeks.
