What changes the price

  • Design. A ready theme is cheaper. A custom design takes more time and makes you look different from competitors.
  • Content. Who writes the text and makes the photos? Content is often the slowest part of any project.
  • Features. Payments, bookings, multi-language, filters and integrations each add work.
  • Products. Uploading 30 products and 3,000 products are very different jobs.
  • Speed and SEO. A fast, search-ready site needs extra care: image optimization, clean code, schema and redirects.

Yearly costs people forget

The build is only the start. Budget for these every year:

  • Domain: about ৳1,200 - ৳2,500 for a .com, more for .com.bd.
  • Hosting: ৳3,000 - ৳20,000 for shared or cloud hosting. Shopify has its own monthly plan instead.
  • Plugins and apps: premium themes, backups, security and booking tools.
  • Maintenance: updates, backups and small changes. Unmaintained sites get slow and hacked.
  • Payment gateway fees: bKash and SSLCommerz charge a small percentage per transaction.

Cheap websites that cost more

A ৳8,000 website is tempting. It often ends with slow pages, no mobile layout, no access to your own hosting, and a rebuild a year later. Before you sign, check three things:

  1. You will own the domain, hosting and admin login.
  2. The site loads in under 3 seconds on a phone on mobile data.
  3. Basic SEO is included: titles, descriptions, sitemap and Google Search Console.

How to get an accurate quote

Send any agency the same short brief: your goal, the pages you need, the features you need (payments, booking, languages), example sites you like, and your deadline. You will get comparable quotes instead of guesses.
